Authors: Alex Lupsasca et al. | Affiliation: Harvard / OpenAI
Abstract: This work demonstrates how large language models can assist in solving complex theoretical physics problems. The research team used AI to crack a gluon scattering problem that had stumped physicists for a year.

研究背景：胶子散射振幅的计算是量子场论中的核心问题，传统方法面临计算复杂度指数级增长的挑战。

学术价值：这项工作展示了AI在基础科学研究中的新角色——不仅是计算工具，更是发现伙伴。

Qwen3.5 Technical Report: Hybrid Attention + Sparse MoE at Scale

Authors: Qwen Team | Affiliation: Alibaba Cloud
Abstract: We present Qwen3.5-397B-A17B, an open-weight multimodal language model featuring hybrid linear attention and sparse Mixture-of-Experts architecture.

技术方法：

  • 混合线性注意力：结合全局注意力和局部线性注意力，平衡长序列建模能力与计算效率
  • 稀疏MoE：397B总参数中仅激活17B，实现高效推理
  • 多语言训练：覆盖201种语言的高质量语料

实验结果：在多项基准测试中达到开源模型SOTA，解码吞吐量较Qwen3-Max提升8.6x-19.0x。
