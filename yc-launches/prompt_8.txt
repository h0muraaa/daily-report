项目标题: jo 1.0 - a personal AI that actually knows you
一句话简介: Less "chatbot" and more "a second brain that does things."
YC 批次: Winter 2024
标签: Artificial Intelligence, Consumer, AI, AI Assistant

创始人自述/产品详情:
**TL;DR -** [jo](https://askjo.ai) is a personal AI that runs on your Mac and a private cloud machine we host for you. It knows your life, improves itself autonomously, chats via  Telegram and WhatsApp, and never shares your data. Less "chatbot" and more "a second brain that does things."

<https://youtu.be/7u6vxYF44rI>

Hey YC! We're [Kevin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/liveink/) and [Pradeep](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pradeepelankumaran/), co-founders for a decade and consumer builders since the mid-2000s. For the last few years, we’ve iterated on an experience that’s zero compromise. It’s easy to use and gives consumers control over their privacy. 

🧠 **jo knows you.** jo improves over time as it learns your preferences, kids' schedules, travel habits, and work context. It reviews its own notes nightly and gets better as you use it. We’ve found the continuity over time to be immensely useful.

🏠 **Your machine, your data.** Every jo macOS desktop app user also gets a dedicated cloud machine and storage. Personal data (photos, local files) never leaves your Mac without approval. Powerful cloud inference runs the best available models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Grok, Kimi with more on the way) on top of cloud providers that are barred from training on your data. 

⚡ **10x faster, everywhere you already are.** Voice on your Mac. Text & voice on  Telegram and Whatsapp. Consistent  memory and personality, 24/7.

**What it does**

* On your Mac, jo notices your browsing patterns and group chats, then clips related content (runs locally, doesn’t leave your Mac).
* Sends morning briefings with weather, calendar, and reminders pulled from Gmail and Google Calendar, continuing on from where you left off the night before.
* Controls a real browser with anti-fingerprinting and a high success rate to check availability, compare prices, and research.
* Searches across every messaging platform conversation(including groups) and email to find things you've forgotten.
* Runs background automations you set once: "Every Thursday, send me a ideas for what to do this weekend"
* jo will not send emails or messages, but will draft and suggest your emails (Gmail).

 

## **The self-organizing digital you**

* Unlike other AI assistants, jo self improves to maximize  usefulness and fun.
* Scan back as many months of your browser and messenger history as you want for jo to work with. 
* Scheduled and autonomous recurring work compounds data and preferences, all stored on machines you have access to with strict exfil policies.
