项目标题: Lucent - AI that automatically watches your session replays 
一句话简介: An AI that watches your session replays 24/7 to detect bugs and UX issues - delivering alerts in real time.
YC 批次: Winter 2026
标签: Developer Tools, B2B, AI

创始人自述/产品详情:
**The problem:** 

* You are collecting a lot of session replays but no one has time to watch them 
* Users are silently hitting bugs in your product, and most of them don’t report bugs, they just churn

**The solution:** 

Lucent is an AI that watches your session replays 24/7, and automatically detects issues your users are running into in real time

**How it works:** 

1. Connect your PostHog account
2. AI watches session replays 24/7
3. Bugs get sent to your Slack or Linear as users encounter them
