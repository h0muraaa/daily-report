项目标题: Scheduling Wizard: Logistics infrastructure to modernize healthcare operations
一句话简介: Beginning with physician scheduling and care coordination
YC 批次: Winter 2026
标签: Artificial Intelligence, Health Tech, Scheduling

创始人自述/产品详情:
Hey YC! We’re Ab, Sam and Zac.

**We are building the logistics infrastructure to modernize healthcare operations, beginning with physician scheduling and care coordination, to combat rampant inefficiencies in healthcare.**

**Our Beginning:**

This all began when a doctor at Johns Hopkins told us that residents and attending physicians spend **hundreds of hours** each year scheduling. Thus began our **physician** **scheduling service**. 

We built an internal **Optimization Programming Language** that lets us create complex schedules far faster than traditional methods. Our clients now review schedules we build, rather than spend hundreds of hours manually creating them.

Through our scheduling, we have built trust in healthcare logistics across major academic health systems with active contracts at **Mass General**, **Johns Hopkins**, **UT Southwestern**, **LA General**, **UCF** and more! We are actively growing and onboarding new partners.

**Revelations I:**

These relationships revealed that healthcare administration, more generally, is unbelievably manual, disjointed, and inefficient. That’s why we have expanded our scope to tackle clinical care coordination. We have separated clinic operations into two segments: **The Patients**, and **The Resources**. 

**The Patients:**

The patient journey at most clinics begins with a referral process that leads to a triaging bottleneck before a patient can be seen. Only once this is complete can the complex systems for evaluation and treatment tracking begin.

**The Resources:**

Managing and tracking personnel, rooms, and time is difficult for even the best administrative teams.

**The Secret**

The problem with healthcare is not on the demand side, it is on the supply side. **Care coordination is the bridge between patient needs and hospital resources.**

**Revelations II:**

With our design partners at **UCSF and other major healthcare systems**, we are building patient triaging and provider management systems to improve and automate care coordination, while reducing administrative burden.

We aim to modernize healthcare operations. As our partnerships grow and our scope expands, we will become the key player in **bringing healthcare operations into the future.**
