Architects sit at the center of a $300B industry with 3.5M professionals globally, yet their core workflows remain largely untouched by AI. \*\*Launch Video: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0C4puetlV8>\*\*\

**The Problem**\
\
Architecture sits at the center $12T construction ecosystem. Yet the workflows that run architectural projects — specifications, documentation, coordination, and compliance — are still largely manual.

Architects spend huge amounts of time writing specs, organizing project documentation, coordinating materials, and checking drawings across fragmented tools.

Despite the scale of the industry, there are virtually no AI-native solutions built specifically for architectural workflows.\
\
**The Solution**

**Avoice is building AI operating system for architecture.**

Our AI agents automate the operational workflows that run architectural projects — generating specifications, structuring project documentation, coordinating materials, and managing schedules.

Instead of manually assembling documentation across multiple systems, architects can run these workflows through Avoice turning weeks of works into minutes.

Today firms are already using Avoice to run real projects.

Over $300M in construction projects are currently operating on the platform across firms in 5 countries.\
