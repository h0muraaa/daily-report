项目标题: GRU Space - The Moon Factory
一句话简介: GRU's Moon Factory will build the world's first bricks on the Moon & permanent lunar infrastructure.
YC 批次: Winter 2026
标签: Space Exploration, Construction, Travel, Aerospace

创始人自述/产品详情:
In just 6 weeks, GRU Space built the world’s first [Moon Factory](https://youtu.be/k9X7TuAxNrw) for under $5,000. GRU’s patent-pending prototype autonomously: 

• mines lunar soil (regolith)  

• refines it using a geopolymer process  

• turns it into structural bricks to build lunar infrastructure GRU has also built a patent-pending modular inflatable habitat designed to withstand the temperature and pressure extremes on the Moon. 

Combining with the Moon Factory, it forms the basis of the first lunar hotel, Moon base, and ultimately, permanent lunar infrastructure. 

Before GRU, lunar habitats can only be built on Earth. At hundreds of thousands of dollars per kilogram, that makes large-scale lunar bases nearly impossible. 

Humanity has not built a product on the Moon yet. At GRU, we are changing that. 

As early as next year, GRU will land a small-scale version of their factory & inflatable on the Moon. 

Humanity cannot become interplanetary until we solve off-world surface habitation. Once solved, it will become the Promethean moment that will enable billions of human lives to be born on the Moon and Mars.
