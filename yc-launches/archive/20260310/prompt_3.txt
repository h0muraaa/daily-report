Building hardware is hard enough. Having to think about compliance too is just overkill. Noetic streamlines the entire certification and testing process using AI agents, making it **significantly faster and cheaper** than legacy consultants.

**The Problem**

* Hardware compliance is scattered, complicated, and opaque
* Teams spend months and tens of thousands of dollars before ever even testing
* Critical information is scattered across thousands of pages of regulations and standards
* Mistakes lead to months in delays, expensive re-testing, and lost deals

**The Solution**

* Give us your product specs and target markets
* Our AI agents analyze thousands of pages of standards and case studies specific to your product
* We compile the necessary technical files and test plans, industry professionals review and sign off
* Your product is sent to the right NRTL/accredited testing lab in our network
* Track progress through a dashboard instead of having to get hands-on
* Within weeks, you have a certified product and the documentation needed for customers and regulators to approve deployment. And all you had to do was send some documents
