项目标题: Bluma - AI UGC Maker
一句话简介: Clone winning organic and paid ad video/slideshows
YC 批次: Fall 2025
标签: B2B, Social Media, Video, Marketing, Advertising

创始人自述/产品详情:
Hey YC!

We're Alisa and Stephen.

Super excited to introduce Bluma - the all in one platform for AI UGC that clones winning organic and paid ad video/slideshows.

It allows you to remix content with a built in video editor and node-based canvas workflows.

We are the first to crack de-editing - cutting each scene, removing and then adding back on caption, text and on screen image popups.

No need to switch between different models and creative platforms to compile clips/assets.

**Problem**

UGC is very time consuming and requires recruitment, week long back and forths and expensive retainers/per video fees.

Oftentimes for both organic and paid, the best way to find a format is to copy adjacent competitors and remake their script/visuals exactly.

The whole process end to end is extremely long and expensive, ranging from researching, briefing, sourcing, reviewing, editing, making variations and distributing.

**Solution**

We believe that AI footage is getting and will continue to get so good that both will converge and be indistinguishable. As such, we can help bring together the whole marketing stack into one platform where all you need is a really good prompter and taste.

![uploaded image](/media/?type=post&id=98704&key=user_uploads/2724981/abcdeafe-fa89-4ddc-a571-70e9c7b880e5)

Let's say you want to recreate a winning UGC skincare ad. You can first take the URL and automatically de-edit each scene, captions, text and photo overlays. Then, you can use our asset maker to recreate all the shots exactly.

Then you can save all your workflows as templates in our node based canvas.

![uploaded image](/media/?type=post&id=98704&key=user_uploads/2724981/758866c5-48a3-47c4-b396-723ed16f203f)

We allow you to split screen to work between assets and editing seamlessly - including a creative agent and capabilities like auto captioning.

![uploaded image](/media/?type=post&id=98704&key=user_uploads/2724981/dc015a6b-b975-47ed-94bf-ae78dfe24ae2)

You can also clone slideshows and create multiple variations in one canvas.
