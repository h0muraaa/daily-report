项目标题: 🚀 Gauge: Your marketing agent for organic, paid, and AI search
一句话简介: A single agent that can do the work of an entire marketing team
YC 批次: Summer 2024
标签: SaaS, B2B, Analytics, Marketing, AI

创始人自述/产品详情:
With user behavior spreading across traditional and AI search, it’s never been more important to ensure that your brand is the answer. There’s a wealth of data distributed across GA4, GSC, keywords, prompts, ads, and more. This data is incredibly rich, but fragmented and complicated to understand.

Gauge unifies all of these data sources into a single agent that can do the work of an entire marketing team. Here’s how it works:

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SxdKlv--Oeo>

We started by building a best-in-class GEO/AEO platform, with a core focus on getting the right data. We quickly found that data alone isn’t enough. Our users kept asking:

1. How can I make sense of all of this prompt/answer data?
2. How does it relate to my existing data?
3. What are the actual things I need to do to move the needle?

Our agent interprets all of your data sources and executes high-value work for you. Gauge can run keyword research, analyze the competition, and track prompts over time for you. Once Gauge has all the data it needs, it can build and execute plans of attack, recommending and drafting focused content. Gauge also lives where you already work, with MCP and Slack bot support.

Gauge is already a core part of the marketing stack at some of the best YC companies - Supabase, PostHog, Mux, and more.
