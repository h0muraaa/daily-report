**TL;DR:** ClawGTM turns your website URL into an autonomous sales force. Our OpenClaw agents identify your best-fit leads, perform deep contextual research, and start conversations on Email and LinkedIn, all in under 60 seconds.

### **The Problem**

Most founders and sales teams are stuck in a manual loop. You spend hours hunting for leads and stalking LinkedIn profiles, only to send "personalized" emails that still feel like templates.

Existing "AI SDRs" have made this worse. They've flooded inboxes with generic, robotic spam that prospects have learned to ignore. If your outreach doesn't have **real reasoning** behind it, it’s going straight to the bin.

### **Meet ClawGTM: Your Autonomous GTM Engine**

ClawGTM doesn't just "automate" emails; it **deploys intelligence**. Built on the viral OpenClaw framework, our agents perform the heavy lifting of a full sales team:

* **Website as a Prompt:** Paste your URL. The agent reads your site to understand your core value prop and target audience.
* **Hyper-Detailed ICP Filtering:** The agent performs deep, multi-layered filtering to find prospects in specific niches that traditional tools miss.
* **Job-Based Intelligence:** The agent monitors **hiring patterns** and **leadership changes** to infer what a company is dealing with. If they are hiring for "Postgres DBA" or "Compliance Officer," the agent identifies the specific tools they use and the internal hurdles they are currently facing.
* **Multi-Channel Execution:** Once the niche is identified, it coordinates across Email and LinkedIn with 1-of-1 messaging that reflects your product's specific value.
