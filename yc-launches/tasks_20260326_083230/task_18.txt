项目标题: Ventura - AI Teammates for Industrial Distributors
一句话简介: We automate quoting and order processing. Distributors win more business.
YC 批次: Winter 2026
标签: 

创始人自述/产品详情:
Hey everyone, we’re [Jack](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jackmpcollins) and [Swen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/swenkoller/) - cofounders of [Ventura](https://www.ventura.ai) 👋

**TL;DR**

Industrial distributors spend billions of dollars a year on inside sales teams whose primary job is manually typing quotes and orders into ERPs. Ventura automates that work. Reps go from doing data entry to approving AI-generated quotes. Response times drop from hours to minutes, and distributors win more business.

<https://youtu.be/eKK1mMQ6_Ss>

**❌ The Problem**

Industrial distribution is an $8 trillion industry. It keeps the construction industry supplied and factories running. But the tools running it haven't kept up.

A typical distributor gets hundreds of quote and order requests every day - over email, PDF, Excel, handwritten fax, and phone. Every one has to be manually read, interpreted, and typed into the ERP. By a human.

The numbers:

* One distributor we work with processes more than 1M quotes and sales orders per year, almost entirely by hand
* 35% of B2B deals go to whoever responds first
* One of our customers estimates the delay costs them millions in lost deals every year

The best salespeople in the industry spend their days on data entry instead of selling.

**✅ The Solution**

![uploaded image](/media/?type=post&id=98814&key=user_uploads/490571/c00a0929-5c5a-46e6-81e6-6021bb6f1f0e)

Ventura plugs into the ERP distributors already run on. Most customers are live within days. Ventura interprets the incoming request, drafts a quote or order, and provides an interface for reps to review Ventura’s output. The result then gets submitted to the ERP.

Two things customers love about Ventura:

* **It learns about your customers and products.** A distributor might carry 150 types of nails. When a buyer writes "the usual nails," Ventura goes back through that customer's email history and makes a judgment call on the right SKU. And it gets sharper with every example, just like a smart teammate.
* **You can teach it new skills and processes.** We start with quoting and order entry. One customer then taught Ventura to convert construction spec sheets into material lists by writing down exactly how their best rep handles it. That task - previously 30 minutes per document - now takes seconds. Any workflow your team does repeatedly can become a Ventura skill.
