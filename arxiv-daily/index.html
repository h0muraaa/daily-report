Timing and Reliability Limits of Position-Assisted UWB Multicast Audio

Fengyun Zhang

One-to-many ultra-wideband (UWB) transport can disseminate shared audio blocks without replicating a downlink stream for every receiver. Yet common reception does not ensure aligned electrical playback, coincident acoustic arrival, or delivery to an entire receiver group. This paper develops a unified feasibility analysis of position-assisted UWB multicast audio that separates clock and actuation error, age-dependent acoustic propagation compensation, correlated block erasures, and reserved communication resources. We derive a conditional bound on one-listener arrival spread, a stationary expression for all-receiver block delivery under common and receiver-local losses, and complementary bounds for reliability-constrained admission. Reproducible Monte Carlo experiments examine 4,590 policy--run records across clock, localization, channel, and slot configurations. For eight receivers, affine clock correction produces a mean run-level 95th-percentile electronic span of $21.19\us$, while position assistance reduces the corresponding one-listener acoustic arrival span from $21.95\ms$ to $419.43\us$. Under the stated $30\us$ timing criterion, 98.86\% of compensated events still exceed the threshold. For 64 receivers, three block copies attain an all-receiver delivery probability of approximately 0.97478 under the modeled correlated channel. The analysis exposes the distinct failure mechanisms of position age, shared fades, retransmission limits, and schedule saturation, and establishes joint criteria for interpreting synchronous multicast audio performance.

SKAD: Sketch-Guided Audio-Contrastive Decoding for Reasoning in Large Audio-Language Models

Junyu Wang, Peiyuan Jiang, Siyuan Zhang 等9人

Large Audio-Language Models (LALMs) have shown increasing potential in audio understanding and reasoning by generating intermediate reasoning steps before answering. However, directly eliciting long-form reasoning from audio does not always lead to more reliable predictions. Even reasoning-oriented LALMs may gradually shift from acoustic evidence to language-continuation patterns during generation. A natural solution is to use contrastive decoding with an audio-masked branch, but directly applying this strategy to plain chain-of-thought reasoning is often ineffective, since the masked branch lacks sufficient audio-related context and provides an unstable reference. To address this issue, we propose \textbf{SKAD} (\textbf{Sk}etch-Guided \textbf{A}udio-Contrastive \textbf{D}ecoding), a training-free inference-time method for audio reasoning. SKAD first constructs an acoustic evidence sketch that summarizes question-relevant auditory information. It then performs contrastive decoding between an audio-conditioned reasoning branch and a sketch-only branch. By using the sketch as a shared semantic scaffold, SKAD makes the masked branch more informative and encourages decoding decisions that are more dependent on the original audio. We evaluate SKAD on Qwen2.5-Omni-7B and Step-Audio-R1 across three audio understanding and reasoning benchmarks: MMAR, MMAU, and MMSU. Experimental results show that SKAD consistently improves the average accuracy of both models across all benchmarks. Further ablations show that direct contrastive decoding without the acoustic evidence sketch provides limited or negative gains, confirming the importance of constructing a reliable contrastive reference before audio-contrastive decoding.

Transformer-Based Plate Parameter Estimation with Differentiable and Particle-Swarm Refinement

David Marttila, Rodrigo Diaz, Pablo Tablas De Paula 等5人

We present two Transformer-based methods for Task A of the 1st DAFx Parameter Estimation Challenge, which requires estimating six effective physical parameters of a synthetic plate-reverb model from its impulse response (IR). Method A1 combines an Audio Spectrogram Transformer encoder and Transformer regressor with differentiable IR refinement. Method A2 uses the same encoder to condition a continuous normalizing flow and refines sampled candidates using particle swarm optimisation (PSO) and gradient polishing. Both methods preserve the absolute IR scale to recover surface density. On a synthetic holdout set of 100 IRs, both refinement procedures reduce waveform and parameter errors by more than three orders of magnitude relative to the unrefined neural outputs. The PSO-based pipeline achieves the lowest errors, indicating near-perfect recovery in this matched synthetic setting.

Sound Matching with a Differentiable Karplus-Strong Algorithm

Pablo Tablas de Paula, David Marttila, Rodrigo D\'iaz 等6人

We present a self-supervised, event-based sound matching model using a differentiable extended Karplus-Strong algorithm. To avoid relying on external onset and fundamental frequency detectors, we explore training methodologies combining parameter losses on synthetic data with audio losses. We demonstrate that time-domain fractional delay interpolation provides gradient accuracy comparable to frequency-sampling while avoiding time-aliasing in highly resonant time-varying scenarios. Through systematic gradient analysis, we reveal that standard spectral losses provide no meaningful directional gradients for onset times, heavily degrading joint training. Training exclusively with parameter losses on synthetic data effectively learns fundamental frequency, timbral parameters, and onset times, but struggles to generalise to monophonic studio recordings of plucked guitar. External detectors combined with audio losses generalise best, isolating the model to timbre optimisation. While our Karplus-Strong decoder recovers interpretable parameters and naturally captures the transient characteristics of plucked guitar, Harmonics plus Noise baselines yield higher reconstruction fidelity by most metrics.

VISH-ASR: Viterbi-Guided Selection of Hotwords for Speech-LLM Contextual ASR

Xiaoyu Gu, Yu Xi, Chencheng Wang 等7人

Prompt-based contextual ASR requires selecting a compact hotword subset from large dynamic inventories, yet existing pre-selection methods rely either on discrete ASR hypotheses that may discard acoustic evidence or on separately trained retrieval models. We propose Viterbi-guided Selection of Hotwords (VISH), a training-free, plug-and-play framework that directly verifies candidate-specific acoustic support over frame-level CTC posteriors. VISH formulates pre-selection as constrained posterior search; M-VISH makes large-list verification scalable through frame-synchronous shared-prefix Viterbi search with bounded top-K pruning, while Bi-MVISH exploits complementary backward evidence for robust verification. Across five benchmarks, M-VISH achieves up to 11.7% absolute improvement in Best-F1 over a strong learned-retrieval baseline and reduces candidate-search latency by 75.2% at 2,000 candidates relative to hypothesis-based filtering. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of posterior-level verification for accurate and scalable hotword pre-selection without additional model training.

AD-Mamba: Architecture-Complementary Knowledge Distillation for Efficient and Robust Audio Classification

Junyu Wang, Tianrui Wang, Meng Ge 等6人

Recent advancements in large-scale audio classification have been primarily driven by Transformer-based architectures due to their strong capacity in modeling long-range dependencies. However, their inherent quadratic complexity limits scalability and efficiency, especially when processing long or sparsely informative audio streams. Mamba, a recently proposed state space model (SSM), offers a promising alternative by enabling efficient long-range modeling with linear sequence scaling. Nevertheless, existing Mamba-based audio classification methods still lag behind mainstream Transformer-based models, suggesting that effective training strategies for fully exploiting the audio modeling potential of SSMs remain underexplored. To address this issue, we propose AD-Mamba, an architecture-complementary multi-stage knowledge distillation framework that transfers the local modeling strength of CNNs and the global contextual modeling capability of Transformers into a Mamba-based student model. Specifically, the CNN-guided stages encourage the student to adapt to local spectro-temporal structures, while the Transformer-guided stage further refines global semantic discrimination and contextual modeling. Experimental results on AudioSet show that AD-Mamba achieves 50.3% mean average precision (mAP) with only 30M parameters, outperforming representative Transformer-based and Mamba-based audio classification baselines with lower computational cost. Further evaluations under long-form audio, sparse-information, and noise-interference conditions show that AD-Mamba consistently outperforms representative CNN and Transformer baselines. When transferred to FSD50K, ESC-50, and DCASE16, the proposed model also achieves superior performance, demonstrating strong generalization across audio classification tasks.

RealComm: Benchmarking and Adapting Audio Deepfake Detection over Real Communication Channels

Li Wang, Jindong Wang, Wan Lin 等12人

Acoustic capture, device processing, and transmission coding in real calls jointly alter speech signals and may affect the reliability of audio deepfake detection. To systematically evaluate these effects, we introduce RealComm, a dataset pairing digital speech with recordings of the same utterances transmitted through real mobile calls. The acquisition design covers acoustic and wired injection, handset and speakerphone operation, and caller-side denoising, with bona fide and synthetic speech recorded under matched conditions. The dataset includes RealCommBench for evaluation and RealCommTrain for adaptation. We further develop RealComm-Aug, a staged waveform augmentation pipeline for simulating acquisition and transmission effects, and investigate its combination with real-call training. Experiments with existing detectors reveal substantial degradation after call transmission, even for models with strong digital-domain performance; acoustic injection is particularly challenging. Paired quality and spectral analyses show that quality degradation is associated with poorer detection across recording conditions for the evaluated detectors, while high-frequency attenuation alone does not explain detection difficulty. Simulated augmentation and real-call training provide complementary benefits, with their combination achieving the lowest call-domain error for each of three evaluated detectors. For Teffic-Audio, the equal error rate decreases from 37.94% to 22.37%. The demo and benchmark access information are provided through the project repository.

pyFDN: A Python Toolbox for Feedback Delay Network Design, Analysis, and Optimization

Sebastian J. Schlecht, Facundo Franchino, Jeremy B. Bai

Feedback delay networks (FDNs) provide a compact representation of recursive audio systems for artificial reverberation and decorrelation. Their practical use spans structured matrix design, acoustic decay shaping, numerical simulation, and modal analysis, increasingly combined with differentiable optimization. We present pyFDN, an open-source Python toolbox that brings these operations into a common workflow. The library combines numerical design and analysis routines with block-based time-domain processing and integration with FLAMO for frequency-domain differentiable processing. Explicit network builds and JSON presets connect design, optimization, rendering, and reuse. This paper describes the software architecture, representative capabilities, and reproducibility mechanisms of version 0.5.0, providing a reference for research and teaching that use the toolbox.

Point2Acoustic: Efficient Room Acoustic Parameter Estimation from 3D Geometry and Material Properties

Xun Gao, Raksha Ramakrishna, Tommy Falk 等4人

Scene-adaptive immersive audio rendering requires rapid estimation of acoustic parameters that reflect the surrounding environment. For repeated runtime scene updates, performing a detailed geometric-acoustic simulation for every update can be computationally costly. We present Point2Acoustic, a learning-based framework for direct estimation of six-band reverberation time ($RT_{60}$) and reverberant-to-direct ratio (RDR) from 3D scene geometry and acoustic material properties. Each scene is represented by a surface-sampled point cloud with spatial coordinates and frequency-dependent absorption and scattering coefficients, and encoded using PointNet or PointNet++. We train and evaluate the framework on a synthetic furnished-room dataset with varied geometry and acoustic materials, using geometric-acoustic simulation to obtain reference targets. Point2Acoustic achieves accurate multi-band prediction with millisecond-scale inference, and ablation results indicate that explicit global room descriptors and semantic surface labels provide no consistent benefit. The proposed formulation supports efficient scene-adaptive acoustic rendering in extended reality (XR).

ShlokaDhara: A Multi-Speaker Sanskrit Speech Corpus for Learning Meter-Aware Prosody in Text-to-Recitation

Harshal Kausadikar, Onkar Kishor Susladkar, Prathmesh Santosh Choudhari 等6人

Sanskrit verse recitation is governed by $\textit{Chandas}$, a strict prosodic framework defined by rhythmic $\textit{Laghu}$ (light) and $\textit{Guru}$ (heavy) syllables. Because existing Sanskrit speech datasets prioritize ASR or conversational TTS, they lack the metrical fidelity required for authentic verse synthesis. We introduce $\textbf{\textit{ShlokaDhara}}$, a 43-hour, multi-speaker Sanskrit speech corpus capturing diverse meters, recitation styles, and melodic variations. We specifically focus on $\textit{Text-to-Recite}$, a synthesis task explicitly requiring both prosodic accuracy and metrical correctness. Benchmarking state-of-the-art TTS architectures via an automated UTMOS (neural MOS) predictor demonstrates that XTTS-v2 achieves the highest perceptual quality. We further validate the XTTS-v2 generated samples using two paradigms: subjective assessments by 16 Sanskrit experts and novel empirical metrics, specifically, syllable-level $\textit{Laghu-Guru}$ duration analysis to objectively quantify structural metrical awareness. Ultimately, this work establishes a robust dataset and evaluation benchmark for prosody-aware computational Sanskrit recitation. Generated Samples: https://shlokadhara.vercel.app/

PW-Pruning: Phase-Aware Expert Selection for Speech-Centric Omni-Modal Models

Junjie Cao, Fengping Tian, Wenjie Zhou 等8人

Sparse routing reduces per-token computation but leaves the full expert bank resident in memory. We study expert pruning for speech-centric omni-modal models, where pooling calibration statistics can obscure the different expert demands of acoustic-input processing and text-answer generation. We propose PW-Pruning, a phase-aware extension of DO-ACP that constructs separate importance-weighted kernels for acoustic-prefill and teacher-forced answer tokens, then combines phase-weighted log-determinant marginal gains to select one static expert bank. On Qwen3-Omni, retaining 32 of 128 experts per layer improves MMAU-mini and MMSU accuracy over matched pooled selection by 3.20 and 4.38 percentage points, respectively, after supervised fine-tuning, averaged over three runs. Single-phase and shuffled-label controls on this public backbone suggest that semantic phase information contributes to these gains. Experiments on a non-public Qwen3.5-Omni backbone and English-only zero-shot evaluation of Nemotron provide complementary evidence. Across the tested Qwen budgets, AU gains are larger under stronger pruning, without inference-time mask switching.

Disentangling Depression from Cognitive Decline in Elderly Speech Using Concurrent Clinical Assessments

Woori Jeon, Seunghee Ha, Sang-Kyu Lee 等8人

Detecting depression from speech in elderly patients with mild cognitive impairment (MCI) is complicated by overlapping acoustic effects of cognitive decline. Without disentangling these, classifiers risk learning cognitive rather than depression-specific patterns. We present a Korean elderly speech corpus of 89 MCI speakers collected over three years with concurrent depression (SGDS) and cognitive (MMSE) assessments. Both linear mixed-effects models and partial correlations identify the same pattern: of seven feature groups, only formant features are associated with depression after controlling for cognitive function, while widely used F0 features carry no signal. The F1+F2 subset achieves UAR 0.760 with just 12 features, outperforming both the full eGeMAPS baseline and self-supervised representations. These findings show that concurrent clinical assessments can identify interpretable, depression-specific acoustic markers in populations where cognitive and affective symptoms co-occur.

The Impact of Side Information on Voice Privacy

Eulalie Thiombiano, Martha Larson, Vincent Colotte 等4人

Currently, voice-related privacy risks are studied under the assumption that the attacker has access only to spoken audio. In contrast, real-world collections often pair the speech signal with side information; yet to our knowledge, no publicly available dataset provides sufficiently detailed and directly linkable speech and side-information pairs to evaluate the combined risk. We propose a distribution-constrained method to build a semi-synthetic dataset and study singling out risk when geographic side information is assumed to accompany anonymized speech. We instantiate it using the English portion of Mozilla Common Voice and the 2021 UK Census, under ignorant and semi-informed attackers, and introduce a conditional reference that isolates the contribution of the audio beyond the candidate-set reduction induced by the side information. In our controlled geographic case study, for single-utterance speakers, the semi-informed attacker reaches an observed isolation probability of 0.718 in the finest OA-plus-household setting, compared with 0.380 in the signal-only setting; its excess over the conditional reference increases from 0.012 to 0.340. The ignorant attacker shows the same trend at a lower level. The effect is largest at fine granularity and short recordings, precisely the setting in which signal-only evaluation looks safest. Our results show that geographic side information can increase the singling out risk of anonymized speech, and we recommend including attacker settings with side information in future studies of voice privacy.
